## 2. The honest answer on "Ethiopian"

"Ethiopian" is a country, not a language. The viable first languages are:

| Language | Speakers | Script | Diaspora / ARPU | Speech tooling | Verdict |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|
| **Amharic (`am`)** | ~60M | Ge'ez fidel (abugida) | Largest, highest willingness to pay | Azure + Addis production-usable; realtime only Gemini | **Launch first, later** |
| **Tigrinya (`ti`)** | ~9M | Ge'ez fidel | Concentrated, low competition | Weakest | Second, reuses fidel engine |
| **Afaan Oromo (`om`)** | ~45M L1 | Latin (Qubee) | Lower ARPU, thin content | Thin | Third / grant or B2B |
| **Somali (`so`)** | ~25M | Latin | Separate market | Thin | Not an Ethiopian course |

**Recommendation: build the multi-script engine now; do not launch Amharic in
2026.** Reasons, all sourced in `09-ethiopian-languages.md`:

1. **Fidel is a 6-week literacy product, not a language toggle.** 33 consonant
   families × 7 vowel orders = 231 core glyphs plus labialized forms. Reusing
   Hangul's jamo-assembly logic would teach it wrong; only the lesson/drill/SRS
   *structure* transfers.
2. **Speech is not good enough for a speaking-first tutor.** Whisper large-v3
   scores ~151% CER on Amharic; ElevenLabs v3 ~148% WER; no vendor offers
   Amharic pronunciation assessment. Natural-speech ASR is ~28% WER, i.e. about
   one word in four wrong.
3. **The buyer is different.** Korean learners arrive from K-culture; Amharic
   learners are mostly diaspora heritage speakers (parents and adults who
   understand but cannot read fidel). That is a second product with its own
   onboarding, pricing (~$9.99/mo), and community distribution.
4. **The architecture cost is paid now, the content cost is not.** A `Script`
   row with `script_type=abugida`, `composition=abugida`, plus a `LanguagePack`
   that owns normalization/romanization/validation, makes Amharic a data pack
   later rather than a rewrite.

**Cheap probe if you want signal this quarter:** ship a free fidel trainer
(one lesson family + 20 phrases) and a waitlist, with no teacher and no
pronunciation scoring. Gate the full pack on the 10 interviews specified in
`09-ethiopian-languages.md §7.6`.

This is a product decision, not a technical limit. If you want Amharic in the
teacher before Korean is polished, say so and I will sequence it as a
parallel pack — but it will be text-first with pre-generated audio.

## 3. The teacher: ship a lesson, not a chat

The competitive research is unambiguous: "AI you can talk to" is now table
stakes (Duolingo retired Max and put unlimited Video Call in ~$13/mo Super).
The defensible product is a **closed loop inside one 7-minute session**:

```
plan card ("today: refuse politely, 3 ways")
   → learner talks, teacher recasts in flow (≤3 explicit fixes)
   → end: "3 things to fix", each opening a 90-second generated drill
   → drill creates/refreshes FSRS cards with the learner's own sentence
   → next session opens by reusing one of them
   → weekly "mistakes you fixed" + can-do artifact
```

The personal **term library** is the retention surface, not a utility:
tap a word in the transcript (or say "save that"), store
word + romanization + register + source sentence + audio, review with *your*
sentence. Langua is the only close competitor; Speak and Duolingo have none,
and Kiku already has `Item`, `ReviewCard`, and `Phrase` to build on.

Default pedagogy: recast in flow, explicit debrief, cap 2–3 fixes, correction
dial (gentle / balanced / strict). Never show a single pronunciation score.

**What not to build:** avatars, social feed, leaderboards, 6,000-word decks,
open-ended chat as the primary product.

## 4. Real-time stack decision

**Primary: Gemini 3.8 Live (native audio).** Verified on Google's docs today:
it lists both `ko-KR` and `am`, supports tool calls and browser WebSockets, and
prices around **$0.005/min audio input + $0.018/min audio output** before
history re-billing. It is the only vendor that gives a credible path to a
realtime Amharic turn later.

**Fallback / A-B: `gpt-realtime-2.1-mini`.** Verified current; measured
voice-to-voice around 1.0 s p50 in independent tests; 60-minute sessions;
one-minute client secrets.

**Architecture for the MVP:** browser connects directly to the provider with a
**server-minted, short-TTL ephemeral token**; Flask exposes six allowlisted
tools (`get_due_items`, `lookup_dictionary`, `create_drill`, `grade_attempt`,
`add_term_to_library`, `end_session_summary`). No LiveKit/Pipecat until scale;
they add WebRTC and provider-swappability but also a second deployment.

**Non-negotiables:**

- The provider key never reaches the browser; tokens are scoped to one user,
  one language, one session.
- `create_drill` validates generated content against DB item IDs and the
  language pack **before** it is spoken or persisted.
- Raw audio is never stored, matching the current `SpeechAttempt` policy.
- Budget cap per user/day; degrade to text drills at the cap, never hard-stop.

**Cost sanity:** a 12-minute, 40-turn session is roughly $0.56–0.85 on Gemini
Live, ~$0.62 on OpenAI mini. At $11.99–14.99/mo the margin is healthy; naive
per-minute rate-card math understates token-billed realtime by 2–4×.

## 5. P0/P1 model-lifecycle work this exposes

Verified against OpenAI's deprecation page today:

| Model | Shutdown | Impact | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| `gpt-4.1-nano` | **23 Oct 2026 (already gone)** | Translation + conversations broken | **Fixed today** → `gpt-5.6-luna` |
| `tts-1`, `tts-1-hd`, `gpt-4o-mini-tts` | **6 Jan 2027** | All item/phrase/scene audio generation stops | Move behind `TTSProvider`; pick Azure/Gemini/ElevenLabs for batch; no `/v1/audio/speech` replacement exists |
| `gpt-4o-transcribe` | **26 Feb 2027** | Speech checks stop | Move to `gpt-transcribe` / `gpt-live-transcribe` behind `ASRProvider` |
| `gpt-realtime` (old) | 20 Jan 2027 | n/a until teacher ships | Use `gpt-realtime-2.1-mini` |

This is why the provider abstraction (Phase 4) is not academic: two of the
app's three AI surfaces have hard shutdown dates within 14 weeks.

## 6. Sequencing (revised from the 8-phase plan)

The architecture doc's 8 phases are correct but front-load too much schema work
before the teacher ships. Revised order, each independently shippable:

| Sprint | Work | Why now | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0 (done)** | Language core, backfill, P0 chat model | Unblocks everything, fixes a live outage | Low |
| **1** | **TTS/ASR provider boundary** + migrate off `tts-1` | Hard deadline 6 Jan 2027 | Med |
| **2** | **Language packs** (`app/languages/ko/`) + shims | Removes route-level Korean imports; zero behaviour change | Low |
| **3** | **Teacher text MVP** behind `TEACHER_ENABLED` + `TermCard` library | Proves the loop cheaply, no realtime latency/cost risk | Med |
| **4** | **Realtime voice** (Gemini Live), drills → FSRS, transcripts | The differentiator | High |
| **5** | Language-scoped reads/URLs + dictionary API | Needed before language #2 | Med |
| **6** | Contract old columns, soft delete | Only after one release of dual-write | Med |
| **7** | Amharic fidel probe → pack decision | Data-gated, not code-gated | High |

Rule: the teacher is built against `language_id=ko` and the new tables, **not**
against the old `Item.korean` path. That way Phase 5/6 do not rewrite it.

## 7. Pricing and metrics (from the competitive work)

- **Free:** full Hangul + Sound Gym; 5 teacher minutes/day; unlimited term
  capture + SRS (capping the library would kill the moat).
- **Core $11.99/mo or $79/yr:** 20 teacher minutes/day, unlimited drills,
  pronunciation detail, weekly recap.
- **Teacher $24.99/mo:** 45–60 min/day, scheduled calls, study plan.

Instrument: first-session completion ≥70%, 2–3 drills generated per session,
2–3 terms saved per session, ≥40% of saved terms reappearing, D7 teacher cohort
vs non-teacher. Reuse the existing `Event` table; no new warehouse.
