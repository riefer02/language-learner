New here? Start with three moves

Kiku works best as a short daily loop, not a long cram.

  1. Review what is due. Try to recall before revealing. Grading honestly is what schedules the next review.
  2. Speak in a mission or with Teacher. Teacher gives you a short spoken lesson and drills.
  3. Save what you noticed. Use the dictionary or say “save that” to the teacher.
What does “due” mean?

A card is due when its next review time arrives. Kiku schedules that time with FSRS based on how you graded it last time. New cards are capped per day so the queue stays small.
