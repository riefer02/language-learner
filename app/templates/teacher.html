Before your first call

Seven minutes. Microphone only. No raw audio stored.

  1. Allow the microphone when Chrome asks; the call uses it directly.
  2. Say “안녕하세요” and let Kiku lead. Ask for a drill when you want one.
  3. At the end, check the debrief and add drills to your review queue.
What can I ask the teacher?
  • “What does this word mean?” — it searches your dictionary.
  • “Give me two drills for these words.” — it creates short drills.
  • “Save that.” — it adds the term with your sentence.
  • “Slower, please.” — it changes pace without leaving Korean.

Ready when you are. Allow the microphone, then start.
