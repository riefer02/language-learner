Before your first call

  1. Allow the microphone when Chrome asks; the call uses it directly.
  2. Say “{{ speech_language }}” and let Kiku lead. Ask for a drill when you want one.
  3. Use headphones if you can: on speakers Kiku can hear itself and cut its own reply short.
  4. At the end, check the debrief and add drills to your review queue.

What can I ask?

  • “What does this word mean?” — it searches your dictionary.
  • “Give me two drills for these words.” — it creates short drills.
  • “Save that.” — it adds the term with your sentence.
  • “Slower, please.” — it changes pace without leaving the language.
